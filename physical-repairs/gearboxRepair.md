# Repairing the gearbox of the Opal 2

While gearboxes can be pretty easily sourced on eBay, this goes over working on the gearbox you have.

## Tutorial for dismantling the machine

<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qTffBY9GRwQ>

## Parts

- 1x 6002zz bearing ([SKF](https://www.skf.com/sg/products/rolling-bearings/ball-bearings/deep-groove-ball-bearings/productid-6002-2Z))
- 2x 696zz bearings ([SKF](https://www.skf.com/group/products/rolling-bearings/ball-bearings/deep-groove-ball-bearings/productid-619%2F6-2Z))
- 4x 695zz bearings ([SKF](https://www.skf.com/group/products/rolling-bearings/ball-bearings/deep-groove-ball-bearings/productid-619%2F5-2Z))
- Food-safe low-temperature synthetic NGLI 2 grease ([Super Lube 21030 Synthetic Multi-Purpose Grease](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000XBH9HI) happens to fit these requirements)

> [!CAUTION]
> You cannot use any type of grease in the gearbox. **It must be food safe (NSF H1)**
> Additonally, it must be stable in cold environments (Under -40F), and must be plastic and rubber safe.
> It must also be NLGI 2 viscosity

## Bearings

The bearings in the GE Opal 2 are known to catastrophically fail, taking the machine with them.
This can be mitigated (to some extent) by replacing the bearings with high quality ones.

If you have the gearbox open, you might as well replace them all.

[The original poster](https://www.reddit.com/r/IceChewersAnonymous/comments/1hxlbkg/fixing_the_opal_20/) used UXCell brand bearings from amazon, but they failed after 10 months.

It is reccomended you use high quality bearings.
Reputable companies include [SKF](https://www.skf.com/us/products/bearings), [NSK](https://www.nsk.com/am-en/), [TIMKEN](https://www.timken.com/engineered-bearing-solutions/), [NTN](https://bearingfinder.ntnamericas.com/), and [FAG](https://www.schaeffler.us/us/products-and-solutions/industrial/)
