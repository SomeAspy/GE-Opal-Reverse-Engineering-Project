# Connectors

## Tab always on left

### AC Header (Black)

JST VHR 3-pin connector (No center pin)

- LINE (Black)
- N/A
- NEUTRAL (White)

### Compressor Header (Red)

JST VHR 3-pin connector (No center pin)

- NEUTRAL (White)
- N/A
- LINE (Red)

### Motor Header (White)

JST VHR 3-pin connector (No center pin)

- NEUTRAL (RED)
- N/A
- LINE (Black)

### UV Header (Blue)

JST XA 2-pin connector

- 12V DC
- GND
