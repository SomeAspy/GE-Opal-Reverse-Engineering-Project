# Fan replacement

The original fan on the Opal 2 is cheap trash.
I replaced mine with a [Noctua NF-P12 Redux](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07CG2PGY6)
Any standard 120mm computer fan (PWM or not) should work.

Since the machine doesn't have PWM (and doesn't need it, since we are cooling a compressor anyway) only 2 of the 4 connections on this fan are used.

Black is 12V negative, and Yellow is 12V Positive.

Black -> Black

Yellow -> Red

Simply splice those into the original fan connector.

Ensure the fan is blowing _away_ from the machine, so the arrow on the fan should be pointing away from the machine.
